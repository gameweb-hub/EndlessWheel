Endless Wheel

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Endless Wheel ("the game") is developed by Kidaku. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

The short version

Endless Wheel does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no account system, no ads, no analytics, and no internet connection requirement. Everything the game needs to remember stays on your device.

What the game stores on your device

The only thing Endless Wheel saves is your high score, written to a local save file on your device. This value never leaves your device — it is not uploaded, synced, or shared with Kidaku or anyone else.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or account information — there is no account.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases or payment information.

Permissions

Endless Wheel does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or location. If a future update ever changes this, that update's store listing and this page will be revised before it's released, and the change will be described here in plain terms.

Children's privacy

Because Endless Wheel collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds optional cloud saves or ads — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above, before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@kidaku.dev.
